01The Practice

A legal practice built around experience, perspective, and community.

Jennifer L. Homrich is a Monroe County attorney whose legal career has taken her from Michigan to Chicago and back to the community she calls home.

After earning her undergraduate degree from Eastern Michigan University and her Juris Doctor from Thomas M. Cooley Law School, Jennifer built experience across multiple areas of law before establishing her own practice in Monroe.

Today, the Law Offices of Jennifer L. Homrich, PLC provides legal guidance across family law, estate planning, probate, real estate, business law, and civil matters.

03The Attorney

A Monroe connection that runs deep.

Jennifer L. Homrich is a lifelong Monroe County resident who chose to build her legal practice close to the community she knows best.

Her path to practicing law began at Dundee High School, continued through Eastern Michigan University, and led to Thomas M. Cooley Law School, where she earned her Juris Doctor in 2002.

After spending part of her professional career in Chicago, Jennifer returned to the Monroe area and established her own law practice.
